\begin{abstract}
We compute and audit-harden the continuum one-loop ($O(\hbar_B^0)$) two-soliton scattering phase of the \textbf{bad} Boussinesq equation at \textbf{fixed physical momenta} in the \textbf{lattice-matched renormalisation scheme} ($\Delta\mathcal{H}=-\frac{3\hbar_B}{2\pi}u^2$), in the small-amplitude corner ($\beta_1=\varepsilon>\beta_2=r\varepsilon$, $0<r<1$) and at finite amplitudes, in both the overtaking and head-on channels \Lc. First, we show that while all implementations of $\Delta\mathcal{H}$ yield the same one-body fixed-momentum dispersion coefficient $C_{\rm matched}=\frac{1}{2\pi}=C_T$ \Lc, the two-body Faddeev--Korepin frame shift $\delta_{\rm renorm}=\Delta X_1'\Delta P_1+\Delta X_2'\Delta P_2=H_{12}[\Delta P]+D_{12}[\Delta P]$ depends on the one-soliton momentum shift $\Delta P_j$ alone and splits into three distinct prescriptions \Lc: (i) using the lattice-matched canonical collective momentum $p_{\rm can,matched}=\frac{\dd L_{1,\rm matched}}{\dd v}=-\frac{\epsilon}{\pi}\beta+\frac{10\epsilon}{9\pi}\beta^3+O(\beta^5)$ eliminates all $\ln\varepsilon$ infrared divergences, drives the Hirota shift to zero ($H_{12}=O(\varepsilon^2)$), and reduces the scale-invariant Darboux shifts by a factor of $4$ to $D_{\rm ov}[p_{\rm can,matched}](r)=-\frac{2}{\pi}(r^{-1}-r)$ and $D_{\rm ho}[p_{\rm can,matched}](r)=+\frac{2}{\pi}(r^{-1}+r)$ \Lc; (ii) using the fixed-profile mode-sum momentum $p_{\rm mode}$ leaves $H_{\rm ov}[p_{\rm mode}]=\frac{2}{\pi}\ln r\ln\frac{1-r}{1+r}$ and retains a logarithmic $\pm\frac{2}{\pi}(r^{-1}\mp r)\ln\varepsilon$ divergence in $D_{12}[p_{\rm mode}]$ \Lc; (iii) implementing $\Delta\mathcal{H}$ by direct on-shell sound-speed rescaling $c_s=\sqrt{1+2\hbar_B\lambda}$ yields $\Delta_{\rm ct}^{\rm sound}\delta=\lambda(3\delta_0+4P_{0,1}\Delta X_1'+4P_{0,2}\Delta X_2')=O(\varepsilon^2)\to 0$ in both channels \Lc. Second, addressing open problem~\#2, we compute the exact finite-$R$ configuration-space kinetic norm of the time-modulated bad-branch soliton, prove that any local analytic counterterm of order $O(\sinh^5\beta)$ vanishes in $C_{\rm matched}$ and in the $O(1)$ two-body frame shifts, and state the three quantities that remain undetermined on the continuum side \Lc/\Ld. Third, we separate the symmetric and antisymmetric parts under particle exchange: in the directional Bethe/Darboux frame of the Toda comparison, the lattice-matched continuum prediction for the large-$Y$ mixed second difference (rectangle) is $\lim_{Y\to\infty}\mathcal{D}_0(Y)=-2C_{\rm matched}(15\sqrt2-20)=-\frac{15\sqrt2-20}{\pi}\approx -0.386175$, matching the Toda extrapolation $\mathcal{D}_0(32)=-0.39575\pm 0.0217$ within $0.0096$ (whereas the bare continued value $C_{\rm ac}=\frac{2}{\pi}$ gives $-1.544699$, off by a factor of $4$) \Lc. All continuum phase components are tabulated at 55-digit precision in \script{twobody\_cont\_grid.json}.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Good and bad Boussinesq conventions.}
In the repository normalisation (\texttt{AGENTS.md}, \texttt{phase1/review.tex}), the \textbf{good} Boussinesq equation is $u_{tt}=u_{xx}-u_{xxxx}-3(u^2)_{xx}$ with potential $u=\phi_x=2(\ln\tau)_{xx}$ and symplectic form $\Omega=\int\dd\pi\wedge\dd\phi$ ($\pi=\phi_t$). The \textbf{bad} Boussinesq equation, obtained by analytic continuation $\alpha=-i\beta$ ($\beta>0$) and curated in \texttt{phase2/toda\_route.tex} and \texttt{phase2/onebody\_scheme.tex}, is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bad_pde}
u_{tt}=u_{xx}+u_{xxxx}+3(u^2)_{xx},\qquad u=\phi_x=2(\ln\tau)_{xx},
\end{equation}
with supersonic one-soliton parameters ($\epsilon\in\{\pm 1\}$):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bad_onesol}
\begin{aligned}
u_0(x,t)&=-\frac{k^2}{2}\operatorname{sech}^2\frac{k(x-X-vt)}{2},\qquad \Phi(\xi;v,C)=C-k\tanh\frac{k\xi}{2},\\
k&=\sinh\beta,\qquad v=\epsilon\cosh\beta,\qquad \ell=\epsilon\cosh 3\beta,\qquad M=\int_{-\infty}^\infty(-u_0)\,\dd x=2\sinh\beta,\\
\Pi&=-2\epsilon\sinh\beta\cosh\beta,\qquad P_0=\frac23\epsilon\sinh^3\beta\cosh\beta,\qquad E_0=\frac23\sinh^3\beta+\frac{8}{15}\sinh^5\beta,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
and classical collective Lagrangian $L_0(\beta)=vP_0-E_0=-\frac{2}{15}\sinh^5\beta$, which satisfies $\frac{\dd L_0}{\dd v}=P_0$ and $\frac{\dd P_0}{\dd\beta}=\frac23\epsilon\sinh\beta\sinh 3\beta$ \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}, \script{twobody\_cont\_grid.py}).

\paragraph{Bad-Boussinesq / Toda Fermi-edge dictionary.}
From \texttt{phase2/toda\_route.tex} eqs.~(13)--(24) and \texttt{phase2/onebody\_dispersion\_test.tex} eqs.~(30)--(36), the hyperbolic Toda chain with potential $V(r)=e^{-r}+r-1$ at finite background strain $a>0$ ($b=e^{-a/2}$, sound speed $c_s=a\sqrt{b}$) maps in its right-moving plus left-moving continuum limit to the bad Boussinesq equation~\eqref{eq:bad_pde} via \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_grid.py}):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:toda_dict}
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_j&=\sqrt{3}\,\beta_j,\qquad h=3b\,\hbar_B,\qquad \hbar_B=\frac{h}{3b},\\
y_j&=\frac{b\kappa_j^2}{h}=\frac{\beta_j^2}{\hbar_B},\qquad \beta_j=\sqrt{\hbar_B\,y_j},\qquad r:=\frac{\beta_2}{\beta_1}=\sqrt{\frac{y_2}{y_1}},\\
P_B&=\frac{a\,\overline{P}}{6\sqrt{3}\,b},\qquad E_B=\frac{\overline{E}}{6\sqrt{3}\,b^2},\qquad S_T=3b\,S_B\implies \frac{\delta_T}{h}=\frac{\delta_B}{\hbar_B}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Because $S_T/h=S_B/\hbar_B$ holds identically, dimensionless semiclassical scattering phases $\delta(\beta_1,\beta_2)=\hbar_B^{-1}\delta_{0,B}+\delta_{1,B}+O(\hbar_B)$ require \emph{no overall rescaling factor} between the bad Boussinesq continuum ($O(\hbar_B^0)$) and the Toda lattice ($O(h^0)$) \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_grid.py}). In the Fermi-edge scaling regime where $y_j=\beta_j^2/\hbar_B$ is held fixed as $\hbar_B\to 0$, expanding at small amplitude $\beta_1=\varepsilon>\beta_2=r\varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon=\sqrt{\hbar_B y_1}$) organises the continuum phase into a power series in $\varepsilon^2=y_1\hbar_B$.

\section{Bad-Equation Two-Soliton Phase in the Lattice-Matched Scheme (Task 1)}

\subsection{Exact Factored Hirota Ratio and Classical Phase on the Bad Branch}

\begin{proposition}[\Lc, \script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}, \script{twobody\_cont\_grid.py}]\label{prop:bad_classical}
On the bad Boussinesq branch ($k_j=\sinh\beta_j$, $v_j=\epsilon_j\cosh\beta_j$, $w_j=k_j v_j$, $\ell_j=\epsilon_j\cosh 3\beta_j$, $M_j=2\sinh\beta_j$), the single-soliton dispersion relation $P_-(k_j,w_j)=w_j^2-k_j^2-k_j^4=0$ factors a common $2k_1 k_2$ divisor out of the Hirota interaction coefficient $A_{12,b}=-P_-(k_1-k_2,w_1-w_2)/P_-(k_1+k_2,w_1+w_2)$, reducing it to the exact degree-2 form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A12_bad_factored}
A_{12,b}(\beta_1,\epsilon_1;\beta_2,\epsilon_2)
=\frac{\epsilon_1\epsilon_2\cosh\beta_1\cosh\beta_2-1-2(\sinh^2\beta_1+\sinh^2\beta_2)+3\sinh\beta_1\sinh\beta_2}{\epsilon_1\epsilon_2\cosh\beta_1\cosh\beta_2-1-2(\sinh^2\beta_1+\sinh^2\beta_2)-3\sinh\beta_1\sinh\beta_2}.
\end{equation}
Unlike the good branch (which has resonant poles at $\alpha_1\mp\alpha_2=\pi/3$), $A_{12,b}(\beta_1,\epsilon_1;\beta_2,\epsilon_2)>0$ for all $\beta_1\neq\beta_2>0$ in both the overtaking ($\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2$) and head-on ($\epsilon_1=-\epsilon_2$) channels \Lc. For $v_1>v_2$ (which covers overtaking with $\beta_1>\beta_2$, $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2=+1$ and head-on with $\epsilon_1=+1,\epsilon_2=-1$), the classical action phase shift is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta0_bad}
\delta_{0,b}(\beta_1,\epsilon_1;\beta_2,\epsilon_2)
=\frac29(\ell_1-\ell_2)\ln A_{12,b}+\frac23 M_1 M_2(v_1-v_2),
\end{equation}
and in all four sign sectors $(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)\in\{\pm 1\}^2$ its canonical momentum derivatives satisfy identically
\begin{equation}\label{eq:d_delta0_dP}
-\frac{\partial\delta_{0,b}}{\partial P_{0,1}}=\Delta X_1'=\Delta x_{12}+\gamma_1 M_2,\qquad
-\frac{\partial\delta_{0,b}}{\partial P_{0,2}}=\Delta X_2'=\Delta x_{21}-\gamma_2 M_1,
\end{equation}
where $\Delta x_{12}=-\frac{\ln A_{12,b}}{\sinh\beta_1}$, $\Delta x_{21}=+\frac{\ln A_{12,b}}{\sinh\beta_2}$, and $\gamma_j=\frac{\dd\Pi_j}{\dd P_{0,j}}=-\frac{3\cosh 2\beta_j}{\sinh\beta_j\sinh 3\beta_j}$ (independent of $\epsilon_j$).
\end{proposition}

In the small-amplitude corner $\beta_1=\varepsilon>\beta_2=r\varepsilon$ ($0<r<1$), Proposition~\ref{prop:bad_classical} expands as \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Overtaking channel} ($\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2=+1$, $v_1>v_2>1$):
\begin{align}
\frac{A_{\rm ov}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)}{A_0(r)}&=1+r\varepsilon^2+\frac12 r^2\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6),\qquad A_0(r):=\left(\frac{1-r}{1+r}\right)^2,\label{eq:A_ov_small}\\
\delta_{0,b}^{\rm ov}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)&=(1-r^2)\ln A_0(r)\,\varepsilon^2+\left[\frac34(1-r^4)\ln A_0(r)+\frac73 r(1-r^2)\right]\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6).\label{eq:d0_ov_small}
\end{align}
\item \textbf{Head-on channel} ($\epsilon_1=+1,\epsilon_2=-1$, $v_1>0>v_2$):
\begin{align}
A_{\rm ho}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)&=1-3r\varepsilon^2+\left[\frac92 r^2+\frac{13}{4}r(1+r^2)\right]\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6),\qquad
\ln A_{\rm ho}=-3r\varepsilon^2+\frac{13}{4}r(1+r^2)\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6),\label{eq:A_ho_small}\\
\delta_{0,b}^{\rm ho}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)&=4r\varepsilon^2+\frac23 r(1+r^2)\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6).\label{eq:d0_ho_small}
\end{align}
\end{itemize}
Notice that in the head-on channel the Hirota trajectory shift is $\Delta x_{12}^{\rm ho}=-\frac{\ln A_{\rm ho}}{\sinh\beta_1}=+3r\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^3)$, which is $O(\varepsilon^2)$ smaller than the Darboux tail shift $\gamma_1 M_2=-\frac{2r}{\varepsilon}+O(\varepsilon)$, so the leading classical head-on phase $\delta_{0,b}^{\rm ho}=4\beta_1\beta_2+O(\varepsilon^4)$ arises from adding $-\frac43\beta_1\beta_2$ (from $\frac29(\ell_1-\ell_2)\ln A_{\rm ho}$) and $+\frac{16}{3}\beta_1\beta_2$ (from $\frac23 M_1 M_2(v_1-v_2)$) \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}).

\subsection{Phonon Determinant Phase Integrals $I_{12}$ and $J_{12}$}

Following \texttt{phase2/oneloop\_phase.tex}, \texttt{phase2/errata.tex}, and \texttt{phase2/audit\_determinant\_foundations.tex}, let $I_{12}=\frac{1}{4\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}}\theta_1(q)\theta_2'(q)\,\dd q$ be the raw stationary-phase phonon integral and $J_{12}=\frac12(I_{12}-I_{21})$ its antisymmetrised partner. Because the $q=0^\pm$ Levinson endpoint constants on the light good branch ($\alpha_j\in(0,\pi/3)$) are $\theta_j(0^\pm;\alpha_j,\epsilon_j)=\pi(\pm 1+\epsilon_j)$ \Lc\ (\texttt{phase2/audit\_determinant\_foundations.tex}), integration by parts yields \Lc:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:I_J_relation}
I_{12}+I_{21}=-\frac{\pi}{2}(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2),\qquad
I_{12}=J_{12}-\frac{\pi}{4}(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2).
\end{equation}
On the bad branch ($\alpha_j=-i\beta_j$), the three Lax roots are complex conjugates of one real root:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bad_roots}
\kappa_{j,1}=\frac12\sinh\beta_j+i\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cosh\beta_j,\qquad
\kappa_{j,2}=\overline{\kappa_{j,1}},\qquad
\kappa_{j,3}=-\sinh\beta_j,
\end{equation}
with weights $(c_1,c_2,c_3)=(+1,+1,-2)$. Although the bad-Boussinesq vacuum is linearly unstable on the long-wavelength band $|q|\in(0,1)$ (and $|q|\in(0,\sqrt{1+\sinh^2\beta_j})$ around one soliton), the two-body scattering phase integral continued via $\alpha_j=-i\beta_j$ (or integrated directly along real $q\in\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$ using the branch-continuous odd/even decomposition of $\theta_j(q)$ with $t=\sqrt{1+q^2}$) is real and UV-convergent for all $\beta_1\neq\beta_2>0$ \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}).

\begin{proposition}[\Lc, \script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}]\label{prop:bad_det_small}
In the small-amplitude corner $\beta_1=\varepsilon>\beta_2=r\varepsilon$ ($0<r<1$) on the bad branch:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Overtaking channel} ($\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2=+1$): The $r=3,s=3$ real-root sector ($\kappa_{j,3}=-\sinh\beta_j$) reproduces the scale-invariant KdV integral $I_{\rm KdV}(r)$ at $O(1)$, while the complex conjugate root pairs $\kappa_{j,1},\kappa_{j,2}$ contribute starting at $O(\varepsilon^2)$:
\begin{align}
I_{12,b}^{\rm ov}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)&=I_{\rm KdV}(r)+c_{2,\rm det}^{\rm ov}(r)\,\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4),\qquad
J_{12,b}^{\rm ov}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)=\frac{\pi}{2}+I_{12,b}^{\rm ov}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon),\label{eq:I_ov_bad_small}\\
I_{\rm KdV}(r)&=-\frac{4}{\pi}\chi_2(r)-\frac{2}{\pi}\ln r\ln\frac{1-r}{1+r}=\frac{4}{\pi}\int_0^r\frac{\ln x}{1-x^2}\,\dd x,\label{eq:I_KdV_def}\\
c_{2,\rm det}^{\rm ov}(r)&=-\frac{1}{3\pi}\left[2(1-r^2)\ln\frac{1-r}{1+r}+9r\right],\label{eq:c2_det_ov}
\end{align}
where $\chi_2(r)=\frac12[\operatorname{Li}_2(r)-\operatorname{Li}_2(-r)]$. Formula~\eqref{eq:c2_det_ov} is verified to 60 digits against direct 2D quadrature of the $q$-integral and to $O(\varepsilon^4)$ against the exact 9-term dilogarithm sum \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}).
\item \textbf{Head-on channel} ($\epsilon_1=+1,\epsilon_2=-1$): Both $I_{12,b}^{\rm ho}$ and $J_{12,b}^{\rm ho}$ vanish at $O(1)$ and start at $O(\varepsilon^2)$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:I_ho_bad_small}
I_{12,b}^{\rm ho}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)=J_{12,b}^{\rm ho}(\varepsilon,r\varepsilon)
=c_{2,\rm det}^{\rm ho}(r)\,\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4),\qquad
c_{2,\rm det}^{\rm ho}(r)=-\frac{8}{\pi}r.
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Invariance under $\Delta\mathcal{H}$:} Because the lattice-matching counterterm $\Delta\mathcal{H}=\hbar_B\lambda u^2$ ($\lambda=-\frac{3}{2\pi}$) is explicitly $O(\hbar_B)$, it enters the one-loop effective action directly at $O(\hbar_B)$ evaluated on the classical saddle $u_{\rm cl}$ and does not modify the $O(\hbar_B^0)$ quadratic fluctuation operator $\mathcal{D}_v$ or the phonon phase shift $\theta_j(q)$. Thus $I_{12,b}$ and $J_{12,b}$ are unchanged at $O(\hbar_B^0)$ \Lc.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\subsection{The Three Continuum Implementations of the Lattice-Matched Scheme at Two-Body}

In \texttt{phase2/onebody\_scheme.tex} and \texttt{phase2/audit\_onebody\_scheme.tex}, matching the continuum vacuum sound-speed elasticity to the Toda chain fixes the local counterterm
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Delta_H}
\Delta\mathcal{H}=\hbar_B\lambda u^2,\qquad
\lambda=s_{1,T}-s_{1,b,\rm ac}=\frac{3}{4\pi}-\frac{9}{4\pi}=-\frac{3}{2\pi}.
\end{equation}
On a stationary one-soliton background $u_0$, $\int_{-\infty}^\infty u_0^2\,\dd x=\frac23\sinh^3\beta$, so $\Delta\mathcal{H}$ adds $\Delta_{\rm ct}F(\beta)=\frac23\lambda\sinh^3\beta=-\frac{1}{\pi}\sinh^3\beta$ to the one-loop collective energy $F(\beta)=-L_1(\beta)=\Delta E|_P(\beta)/\hbar_B$, shifting \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}):
\begin{align}
F_{\rm ac}(\beta)&=\frac{\sqrt3}{\pi}\left(\sinh^2\beta+\frac34\right)\arctan\frac{2\sinh\beta}{\sqrt3}-\frac{3}{2\pi}\sinh\beta
=\frac{4}{3\pi}\beta^3+\frac{14}{45\pi}\beta^5+\frac{97}{1890\pi}\beta^7+O(\beta^9),\label{eq:F_ac}\\
F_{\rm matched}(\beta)&=F_{\rm ac}(\beta)-\frac{1}{\pi}\sinh^3\beta
=\frac{1}{3\pi}\beta^3-\frac{17}{90\pi}\beta^5-\frac{431}{7560\pi}\beta^7+O(\beta^9),\label{eq:F_matched}\\
C_{\rm matched}&=\lim_{\beta\to 0}\frac{F_{\rm matched}(\beta)}{|P_0(\beta)|}=\frac{1}{2\pi}=C_T\qquad\left(\text{vs.\ }C_{\rm ac}=\frac{2}{\pi}=4C_T\right).\label{eq:C_matched}
\end{align}

As noted in \texttt{phase2/audit\_onebody\_scheme.tex} and \texttt{phase2/audit\_smx.tex}, at the one-body level the fixed-momentum energy shift $\Delta E|_P=\hbar_B(e-vp)=-\hbar_B L_1$ is invariant under any redefinition $(e,p)\mapsto(e+v\delta p,\,p+\delta p)$ because $v\delta p$ cancels out of $e-vp$. At the \textbf{two-body} level, however, converting the fixed-parameter saddle phase into the fixed-physical-momentum scattering phase produces the Faddeev--Korepin frame shift
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta_renorm_def}
\delta_{\rm renorm}(1,2)=-\frac{\partial\delta_0}{\partial P_{0,1}}p_1-\frac{\partial\delta_0}{\partial P_{0,2}}p_2
=\Delta X_1' p_1+\Delta X_2' p_2=H_{12}[p]+D_{12}[p],
\end{equation}
where $P_{\rm phys,j}=P_{0,j}+\hbar_B p_j$, $H_{12}[p]=\Delta x_{12}p_1+\Delta x_{21}p_2$ is the Hirota part, and $D_{12}[p]=\gamma_1 M_2 p_1-\gamma_2 M_1 p_2$ is the Darboux tail part. Equation~\eqref{eq:delta_renorm_def} depends on $p_j(\beta_j,\epsilon_j)$ alone! We therefore evaluate all three candidate implementations of the lattice-matched scheme in \script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}:

\begin{proposition}[\Lc, \script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py}]\label{prop:three_prescriptions}
In the lattice-matched scheme ($\lambda=-\frac{3}{2\pi}$), the three continuum fixed-momentum prescriptions give the following analytical expansions in the small-amplitude corner $\beta_1=\varepsilon>\beta_2=r\varepsilon$ ($0<r<1$):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Prescription C-matched (Canonical collective momentum $p_{\rm can,matched}=\frac{\dd L_{1,\rm matched}}{\dd v}$):}
Since $L_{1,\rm matched}(v)=-F_{\rm matched}(\beta(v))$ is the one-loop effective collective Lagrangian, Noether's theorem on the collective coordinate $X(t)$ defines the canonical momentum shift $p_{\rm can,matched}(\beta,\epsilon)=\frac{\epsilon}{\sinh\beta}\frac{\dd L_{1,\rm matched}}{\dd\beta}$:
\begin{align}
p_{\rm can,matched}(\beta,\epsilon)&=-\frac{2\epsilon\sqrt3}{\pi}\cosh\beta\arctan\frac{2\sinh\beta}{\sqrt3}+\frac{3\epsilon}{\pi}\sinh\beta\cosh\beta\nonumber\\
&=-\frac{\epsilon}{\pi}\beta+\frac{10\epsilon}{9\pi}\beta^3+\frac{2\epsilon}{9\pi}\beta^5+O(\beta^7),\label{eq:p_can_matched}\\
p_{\rm can,ac}(\beta,\epsilon)&=-\frac{2\epsilon\sqrt3}{\pi}\cosh\beta\arctan\frac{2\sinh\beta}{\sqrt3}
=-\frac{4\epsilon}{\pi}\beta-\frac{8\epsilon}{9\pi}\beta^3-\frac{4\epsilon}{15\pi}\beta^5+O(\beta^7).\label{eq:p_can_ac}
\end{align}
More generally, for any smooth $F(\beta)=\frac23 C\beta^3+O(\beta^5)$, the linear slope of $p_{\rm can}$ is universally $c_1(p_{\rm can})=-2C$, and the dressed momentum Jacobian satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:jac_matched}
\frac{\dd(P_0+\hbar_B p_{\rm can,matched})}{\dd P_0}
=1-\frac{C_{\rm matched}}{y}+\frac{5\beta^2}{2\pi y}+O(\beta^4)
=1-\frac{1}{2\pi y}+O(\hbar_B),
\end{equation}
which matches the large-$y$ Toda Bethe Jacobian $u'(y)=1-\frac{1}{2\pi y}+O(y^{-3/2})$ in \texttt{phase2/frame\_dictionary.tex} \Lc\ (\script{twobody\_cont\_det\_antisym.py}). Because $c_{\ln}=0$ in~\eqref{eq:p_can_matched}, \textbf{all $\ln\varepsilon$ infrared divergences vanish}, $H_{12}$ vanishes at $O(1)$, and $D_{12}$ is finite and scale-invariant in both channels:
\begin{align}
H_{\rm ov}[p_{\rm can,matched}]&=-\frac{23}{18\pi}(1-r^2)\ln A_0(r)\,\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4)\xrightarrow{\varepsilon\to 0}0,\label{eq:H_ov_can_m}\\
D_{\rm ov}[p_{\rm can,matched}]&=-\frac{2}{\pi}\left(r^{-1}-r\right)-\frac{1}{3\pi}\left(r^{-1}-r^3\right)\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4)
\qquad\left(\text{vs.\ }-\frac{8}{\pi}(r^{-1}-r)\text{ for }p_{\rm can,ac}\right),\label{eq:D_ov_can_m}\\
H_{\rm ho}[p_{\rm can,matched}]&=-\frac{6}{\pi}r\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4)\xrightarrow{\varepsilon\to 0}0,\label{eq:H_ho_can_m}\\
D_{\rm ho}[p_{\rm can,matched}]&=+\frac{2}{\pi}\left(r^{-1}+r\right)+\frac{1}{\pi}\left[\frac13\left(r^{-1}+r^3\right)-\frac{28}{9}r\right]\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4)
\qquad\left(\text{vs.\ }+\frac{8}{\pi}(r^{-1}+r)\text{ for }p_{\rm can,ac}\right).\label{eq:D_ho_can_m}
\end{align}

\item \textbf{Prescription M (Fixed-profile mode-sum momentum $p_{\rm mode}$):}
Because $\Delta\mathcal{L}=-\Delta\mathcal{H}=-\hbar_B\lambda\phi_x^2$ contains no time derivatives $\phi_t$, the field momentum density $\mathcal{P}=-\pi\phi_x$ is independent of $\lambda$, so at fixed classical profile $\Delta_{\rm ct}p_{\rm mode}=0$ (\texttt{phase2/audit\_onebody\_scheme.tex}). Thus $p_{\rm mode}$ retains its bare expansion
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p_mode_exp}
p_{\rm mode}(\beta,\epsilon)=\epsilon\left(c_{\ln}\beta\ln\beta+c_{\rm lin}\beta\right)+O(\beta^3\ln\beta),\qquad
c_{\ln}=\frac{1}{\pi},\quad c_{\rm lin}=\frac{-\ln 2-1+\frac14\ln 3}{\pi},
\end{equation}

\subsection{Exact Exchange Decomposition in the Symmetric-Cutoff Darboux Frame}

Under full particle exchange $1\leftrightarrow 2$, meaning $(\beta_1,\epsilon_1)\leftrightarrow(\beta_2,\epsilon_2)$ (which sends $r=\beta_2/\beta_1\mapsto 1/r$ in the overtaking channel and swaps the right-mover and left-mover labels in the head-on channel), any two-body phase decomposes into $\delta_1(1,2)=\delta_1^{\rm sym}(1,2)+\delta_1^{\rm anti}(1,2)$ with $\delta_1^{\rm sym}(2,1)=+\delta_1^{\rm sym}(1,2)$ and $\delta_1^{\rm anti}(2,1)=-\delta_1^{\rm anti}(1,2)$.

\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Failures Recorded}

In compliance with \texttt{AGENTS.md}, we list every intermediate assertion failure encountered during the development of the verification scripts and how each was resolved without weakening any check:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py} (Series coefficients of $F_{\rm ac}$ and $F_{\rm matched}$):} Hand-estimated $O(\beta^7)$ coefficient of $F_{\rm ac}(\beta)$ (\texttt{-62/(945*pi)}) and $O(\beta^5)$ coefficient of $F_{\rm matched}(\beta)=F_{\rm ac}(\beta)-\sinh^3\beta/\pi$ (\texttt{-31/(45*pi)}, using $-\beta^5/\pi$ instead of $-\frac12\beta^5/\pi$ from $\sinh^3\beta=\beta^3+\frac12\beta^5+\frac{13}{120}\beta^7$) failed the exact SymPy series assertions and were corrected to $+\frac{97}{1890\pi}\beta^7$ for $F_{\rm ac}$ and $-\frac{17}{90\pi}\beta^5-\frac{431}{7560\pi}\beta^7$ for $F_{\rm matched}$ (giving $p_{\rm can,matched}=-\frac{\epsilon}{\pi}\beta+\frac{10\epsilon}{9\pi}\beta^3+\frac{2\epsilon}{9\pi}\beta^5$).
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py} (Subleading Jacobian term):} The $O(\beta^2)$ term of $\beta^2\frac{\dd p_{\rm can,matched}/\dd\beta}{\dd P_0/\dd\beta}$ was briefly changed from $+\frac{5}{2\pi}\beta^2$ to $+\frac{2}{\pi}\beta^2$ and failed the SymPy assertion at line 203; restored to $+\frac{5}{2\pi}\beta^2$.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py} (Bad-branch $A_{\rm ov}/A_0(r)$ subleading ratio):} Before factoring out $w_j^2-k_j^2-k_j^4=0$, initial test targets $1\mp\frac23 r\varepsilon^2$ and $1+\frac{13}{9}r\varepsilon^2$ for $A_{\rm ov}/A_0(r)$ failed; factoring $A_{12,b}$ into~\eqref{eq:A12_bad_factored} both reduced the script runtime from $\sim 200\,\mathrm{s}$ to $20\,\mathrm{s}$ and proved $A_{\rm ov}/A_0(r)=1+r\varepsilon^2+\frac12 r^2\varepsilon^4+O(\varepsilon^6)$.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py} (Exponent of $r$ in $-\gamma_2 M_1$ for $D_{\rm mode}$):} In \texttt{D\_ov\_mode\_lead} and \texttt{D\_ho\_mode\_lead}, $-\gamma_2 M_1=+\frac{2}{r^2\varepsilon}$ was initially typed as \texttt{2/(r\_s*eps\_s)} instead of \texttt{2/(r\_s**2*eps\_s)}; caught by SymPy expansion assertions and fixed.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_phase.py} (Power of $r$ in $4P_{0,2}\Delta X_2'$ for $\Delta_{\rm ct}^{\rm sound}\delta^{\rm ho}$):} In \texttt{c2\_sound\_ho}, $4P_{0,2}\Delta X_2'$ was initially written as $-\frac{16}{3}r^2\varepsilon^2$ instead of $-\frac{16}{3}r\varepsilon^2$; caught by the SymPy assertion and corrected to $\Delta_{\rm ct}^{\rm sound}\delta^{\rm ho}=-\frac{2}{\pi}r\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4)$, consistent with Euler's homogeneous function theorem for $\delta_{0,\rm ho}^{(0)}=4\beta_1\beta_2\propto |P_{0,1}P_{0,2}|^{1/3}$.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_det\_antisym.py} (Sign of finite kinetic-norm integral):} In the initial draft, $\int_{-\infty}^\infty[(\tanh z+z\operatorname{sech}^2 z)^2-1]\,\dd z$ was written as $-\frac{2(\pi^2-6)}{9}$ instead of $+\frac{\pi^2-6}{9}$ (since $\int_0^\infty(-\operatorname{sech}^2 z+2z\tanh z\operatorname{sech}^2 z)\,\dd z=-1+1=0$, leaving $2\int_0^\infty z^2\operatorname{sech}^4 z\,\dd z=+\frac{\pi^2-6}{9}>0$). Caught by the 70-digit \texttt{mpmath} quadrature assertion at line 61 and corrected.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_det\_antisym.py} ($O(\beta^2)$ coefficient of $F_{\rm matched}/P_0$):} Line 102 initially carried the un-updated coefficient $-\frac{79}{60\pi}$ instead of $-\frac{47}{60\pi}$ from $(1-\frac{17}{30}\beta^2)/(1+\beta^2)=1-\frac{47}{30}\beta^2$; caught by SymPy assertion and corrected.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_det\_antisym.py} (Prefactor of $\lim_{Y\to\infty}\mathcal{D}_0(Y)$ in terms of $C$):} Line 179 initially wrote $-8C(15\sqrt2-20)$ instead of $-2C(15\sqrt2-20)$ (since $8\cdot[-C(15\sqrt2-20)/4]=-2C(15\sqrt2-20)$, which equals $-\frac{15\sqrt2-20}{\pi}$ at $C_{\rm matched}=\frac{1}{2\pi}$). Caught by SymPy limit assertion and corrected.
\item \textbf{\script{twobody\_cont\_grid.py} (Derivative $\dd I_{\rm KdV}/\dd r$, closed-form $\delta_{0,b}$, and good-branch resonance boundary):} (a) Line 260 initially included a non-cancelling boundary term in $\dd I_{\rm KdV}/\dd r$, caught by 55-digit \texttt{mp.diff} check and simplified to $\frac{4\ln r}{\pi(1-r^2)}$; (b) \texttt{delta0\_bad\_mp} and \texttt{dP0\_db\_bad\_mp} initially had transcription typos caught by the 50-digit path-quadrature check on $[0.1,0.2]$ and corrected to~\eqref{eq:delta0_bad} and $\frac23\sinh\beta\sinh 3\beta$; (c) the initial good-branch pairs $(0.8,0.4)$ and $(0.6,0.45)$ had $\alpha_1+\alpha_2>\pi/3$ where $A_{12,g}^{\rm ho}<0$, and were replaced with regular-chamber pairs satisfying $\alpha_1+\alpha_2<\pi/3$; (d) the finite-amplitude convergence check at $\varepsilon=0.1,0.2$ was upgraded to subtract the exact $O(\varepsilon^2)$ term and verify $O(\varepsilon^4)$ scaling (ratio $\approx 16$).
\end{enumerate}

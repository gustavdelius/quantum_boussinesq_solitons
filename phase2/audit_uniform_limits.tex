\begin{abstract}
This document reports an independent mathematical and numerical audit of the
package \scr{phase2/uniform\_limits.tex} (and its compiled \scr{.pdf}) together
with its five verification scripts \scr{uniform\_limits\_analytic.py},
\scr{uniform\_limits\_scan.py}, \scr{uniform\_limits\_wh.py},
\scr{uniform\_limits\_matched.py}, and \scr{uniform\_limits\_refit.py}.
All analytic identities have been re-derived from first principles and all
numerical claims have been re-tested with independent code paths in
\scr{phase2/scripts/audit\_uniform\_limits.py} (symbolic series reversion and
integrand expansion in SymPy, de Hoog--Knight--Stokes Fourier--Pad\'e Laplace
inversion with exact dressed-measure quadrature for the half-line Wiener--Hopf
convolution, a Fej\'er first-rule Chebyshev Nystr\"om solver for the finite-sea
Bethe equations, and independent sensitivity refits of all saved datasets). Every
package script and \scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py} exit cleanly and print
\texttt{ALL CHECKS PASSED}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Executive Summary and Verdict Table}

The package \scr{phase2/uniform\_limits.tex} is a disciplined post-errata
re-assessment of the semiclassical ($h\to 0$) and edge ($Y=b\kappa^2/h$) limits
on the Toda / bad-Boussinesq side. Unlike the earlier Phase~2d--2i packages
corrected in \scr{phase2/audit\_phase2i.tex} and \scr{phase2/errata.tex},
\scr{uniform\_limits.tex} does \emph{not} over-claim a finite-sea resolvent
theorem or a settled finite-sea $1/Y$ two-body phase coefficient. Instead, it
separates unconditional analytic identities from conditional half-line
Wiener--Hopf statements and open finite-sea conjectures \D.

Our independent audit confirms eight of the twelve main claims of the
package, qualifies four claims where script coverage or table summaries require
explicit caveats, and finds no refuted mathematical formulas in
\scr{uniform\_limits.tex} itself (while confirming the package's own refutations
of the earlier Phase~2d step bound and Phase~2f sign-definite $1/Y$ claim).

\section{Mandatory Six-Point Audit Checklist}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Verification of every \C\ claim (\scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py}).}
All \C\ claims in \scr{uniform\_limits.tex} are backed by executable checks, with
three script-coverage gaps identified and closed by our independent script:
\begin{itemize}
\item In \S2, \scr{uniform\_limits\_analytic.py} checked the cusp integral
$\int_{-1}^1 R(z/h)|z|\,dz=\frac{h^2}{3}\log(1/h)+O(h^2)$ only indirectly via the
moment increment $\int_{1000}^{2000}2xR(x)\,dx\approx\frac13\log 2$, and did not
numerically test the operator remainder on a Lipschitz function. In
\scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py} we evaluate the exact $h$-dependent cusp integral
against its three-term asymptotic series
$\frac{h^2}{3}\log(1/h)+2C_{\rm cusp}h^2-\frac{h^4}{60}+O(h^6)$ and test
$\mathcal A_h f(x_0)$ directly on $f(z)=|z-x_0|+\frac12\cos z$.
\item In \S3, \scr{uniform\_limits\_analytic.py} checked the $y\downarrow 0$
limit symbolically only for $C_v(y)$, not $C_E(y)$. In
\scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py} we derive the $s\to\infty$ expansion
$g(s)=1+\frac{\pi}{6s}+\frac{\pi^2}{72s^2}+O(s^{-3})$, obtain
$F(u)=\sqrt{\pi}u+\frac{\pi^{3/2}}{12}u^2+O(u^3)$ and
$J(u)=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}u^2+\frac{\pi^{3/2}}{36}u^3+O(u^4)$, and verify
symbolically that both $C_E(0^+)$ and $C_v(0^+)$ equal $\frac{3}{4\pi}$.
\item In \S4, \scr{uniform\_limits\_matched.py} typed the outer integrands
$I_0(t)$ and $I_1(t)$ in Eqs.~(16)--(17) by hand before integrating them. In
\scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py} we derive $I_0(t)$ and $I_1(t)$ symbolically by
expanding $8Y^{3/2}t^2 f(u(Y)-u(r^2Y)+u(Yt^2))$ in powers of $Y^{-1}$.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Honesty of error budgets.}
The package separates mesh-refinement diagnostics ($N\to 2N$), $h\to 0$
extrapolation spread across multiple polynomial and logarithmic bases and window
lengths, and $y$- or $Y$-basis/window spread, adding the three components
linearly. Our independent refits confirm that no target value ($1/(2\pi)$ or
$c_1(r)$) is fed into any fit. Two qualifications on the error budgets are
recorded in \S\ref{sec:detailed}: (a) the one-body fit subtracts the analytic
half-line tail $-3d/(2y)$ before fitting $(1,y^{-2},\dots)$, which narrows the
$y$-window spread by a factor of $23$ compared with a fully unconstrained
$(1,y^{-1},y^{-2},\dots)$ fit; and (b) in the fixed-$\kappa_1$ table at
$\kappa_1=0.35$, the ``Max.\ mesh diagnostic'' column prints the two-body phase
mesh difference $4.54\times 10^{-9}$ rather than the one-body energy mesh
difference $4.99\times 10^{-9}$.

\item \textbf{Conventions: good/bad dictionary and integrable defect $s$ parameter.}
The project's canonical equation is the \emph{good} Boussinesq equation
$u_{tt}=u_{xx}-u_{xxxx}-3(u^2)_{xx}$ with $u=\phi_x$,
$L=\frac12\phi_t^2-\frac12\phi_x^2+\phi_x^3-\frac12\phi_{xx}^2$,
$\Omega=\int d\pi\wedge d\phi$, one-soliton $k=\sin\alpha$, $v=\varepsilon\cos\alpha$,
$M=2k$, and $P=\frac23\varepsilon k^3\cos\alpha$ \A. The analytic continuation
$\alpha=i\beta$ gives the \emph{bad} equation
$u_{tt}=u_{xx}+u_{xxxx}+3(u^2)_{xx}$ with $k=\sinh\beta$, $v=\varepsilon\cosh\beta$,
and $P_{\rm bad}=\frac23\varepsilon\sinh^3\beta\cosh\beta$ \C.
The package \scr{uniform\_limits.tex} works exclusively in the Toda /
bad-Boussinesq sector with $a=1$, $b=e^{-1/2}$, $h=\hbar_T=3b\hbar_B$,
$P_0(\kappa)=4b(\kappa\cosh\kappa-\sinh\kappa)$,
$E_0(\kappa)=2b^2(\sinh\kappa\cosh\kappa-\kappa)$, and $\kappa=\sqrt{3}\beta$ at
leading cubic order $P_B=P_0/(6\sqrt{3}b)=\frac23\beta^3+O(\beta^5)$ \C.
Regarding the integrable-defect convention audited across Phase~2 (where
scattering transmission uses $s=\sigma_{\rm vac}$ whereas the bound-state
spectrum uses $c=\eta\sigma_{\rm vac}$), \scr{uniform\_limits.tex} does not invoke
defect formulas and never conflates $s$ with $\eta\sigma_{\rm vac}$ \C.

\item \textbf{Absence of circularity.}
We inspected all five scripts for self-comparisons, target injection, or
retroactive tolerance loosening. In \scr{uniform\_limits\_refit.py}, the intercept
$y^0$ (or $h^0$, $Y^0$) and slope $Y^{-1}$ are solved by unconstrained
high-precision least squares (\scr{mpmath.qr\_solve}). In
\scr{uniform\_limits\_wh.py}, $Q(u(Y),u(r^2Y))$ is computed from numerical
contour inversion without using $c_0(r)$ or $c_1(r)$. No circularity is present \C.

\item \textbf{Consistency with earlier audits (\scr{audit\_phase2i.tex}, \scr{errata.tex}).}

and \scr{errata.tex}: it uses the corrected Laplace transform

\section{Detailed Claim-by-Claim Audit}\label{sec:detailed}

\subsection{Section 1: Conventions and Fixed-$P$ vs Fixed-$Y$ Limits}
\paragraph{Claim 1 (\C, Confirmed).}
In \scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py} (\texttt{audit\_conventions\_and\_scales}), we
symbolically verified $P_0'(\kappa)=4b\kappa\sinh\kappa>0$ for $\kappa>0$,
$dE_0/dP_0=b\sinh\kappa/\kappa$, and the small-$\kappa$ expansions
\begin{align}
 P_0(\kappa) &= \frac{4b}{3}\kappa^3 + \frac{2b}{15}\kappa^5 + \frac{b}{210}\kappa^7 + O(\kappa^9), \nonumber\\
 E_0(\kappa) - b P_0(\kappa) &= \frac{2b^2}{15}\kappa^5 + \frac{13b^2}{630}\kappa^7 + O(\kappa^9). \label{eq:E0_minus_bP0}
\end{align}
Setting $\kappa=\sqrt{hY/b}$ at fixed $Y$ yields
$P_0=\frac43 b^{-1/2}(hY)^{3/2}+\frac{2}{15}b^{-3/2}(hY)^{5/2}+O(h^{7/2})\to 0$
as $h\to 0$, confirming that fixed-$Y$ limits shrink the classical momentum
whereas fixed-$P_0>0$ limits send $Y=b\kappa^2/h\propto h^{-1}\to\infty$ \C.

\subsection{Section 2: Bulk Digamma Kernel Boundary Layer}
\paragraph{Claim 2 (\C/\D, Qualified).}
For $K_h(d)=2\operatorname{Re}\psi(1+id/h)-2\log(1/h)=2\log|d|+R(d/h)$ with
$R(x)=2\operatorname{Re}\psi(1+ix)-2\log|x|$, our script
(\texttt{audit\_kernel\_boundary\_layer}) independently verifies:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The exact primitive $G(L)=\int_0^L R(x)\,dx=2\operatorname{Im}\log\Gamma(1+iL)-2L\log L+2L$
to $10^{-61}$ against numerical differentiation and quadrature \C.
\item The non-alternating Bernoulli tail (since $(-1)^m B_{2m}=-|B_{2m}|<0$ for all $m\ge 1$):
\begin{align}
 R(x) &= \frac{1}{6x^2}+\frac{1}{60x^4}+\frac{1}{126x^6}+\frac{1}{120x^8}+\frac{1}{66x^{10}}+O(x^{-12}),\nonumber\\
 G(L) &= \frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{6L}-\frac{1}{180L^3}-\frac{1}{630L^5}-\frac{1}{840L^7}-\frac{1}{594L^9}+O(L^{-11}), \label{eq:R_G_asympt}
\end{align}
and the small-$x$ expansion $R(x)+2\log|x|=-2\gamma+2\zeta(3)x^2-2\zeta(5)x^4+O(x^6)$ \C.
\item \textbf{Direct cusp-integral evaluation (closing a script gap in \scr{uniform\_limits\_analytic.py}):}
Changing variables $z=hu$ gives $\mathcal I_{\rm cusp}(h)=\int_{-1}^1 R(z/h)|z|\,dz=2h^2\int_0^{1/h}uR(u)\,du$.
Splitting at $u=1$ and integrating \eqref{eq:R_G_asympt} yields the exact
asymptotic expansion
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal I_{\rm cusp}(h) = \frac{h^2}{3}\log\frac1h + 2C_{\rm cusp}h^2 - \frac{h^4}{60} + O(h^6),
 \qquad C_{\rm cusp} = \int_0^1 uR(u)\,du + \int_1^\infty u\Bigl(R(u)-\frac{1}{6u^2}\Bigr)\,du \approx -0.2580151499, \label{eq:I_cusp}
\end{equation}
which matches 60-digit quadrature to $4.2\times 10^{-15}$ at $h=10^{-2}$ and
$4.2\times 10^{-21}$ at $h=10^{-3}$ \C.
\item \textbf{Direct Lipschitz operator test:} For $f(z)=|z-x_0|+\frac12\cos z$ at
$x_0=0.2$ on $[-1,1]$, the remainder
$\Delta_h=\mathcal A_h f(x_0)-\bigl[-\int_{-1}^1 2\log|x_0-z|f(z)\,dz+\pi h f(x_0)\bigr]$
satisfies $|\Delta_h|/(h^2\log(1/h))\in\{0.2073,0.2367,0.2571\}$ at
$h\in\{0.02,0.01,0.005\}$, approaching the theoretical cusp coefficient $1/3$ \C.
The package's caveat that this direct-operator estimate does not bound
$\mathcal A_h^{-1}$ up to the endpoints $z=\pm q$ is confirmed \D.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Section 3: Corrected Half-Line Wiener--Hopf Series and Order of Limits}
\paragraph{Claims 3 and 4 (\C\ conditional, Confirmed / Qualified).}
In \scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py} (\texttt{audit\_wh\_reversion\_and\_matched\_proof}),
we independently expand $g(s)=s^{-1/2}\Gamma(1+\frac{s}{2\pi})\exp(-\frac{s}{2\pi}\log\frac{s}{2\pi e})$
as $s\to 0^+$ and apply the Hankel contour identity
$\mathcal L^{-1}[s^{-\nu}\log^m s](u)=(-1)^m\partial_\nu^m[u^{\nu-1}/\Gamma(\nu)]$
to $\mathcal L F(s)=\sqrt{\pi}g(s)/s^2$ and $\mathcal L J(s)=\sqrt{\pi}g(s)/s^3$.
We confirm:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The forward expansion $y(u)=[(3/4)F(u)]^{2/3}$ contains the logarithmic term
$-\frac{1}{16\pi^3}\frac{(\log u)^2}{u^2}$ \C.
\item Series reversion of $y(u)$ yields
$u(y)=y-A\log y+c+\frac{d}{y}+\frac{e}{y^2}+\frac{f_3}{y^3}+O(y^{-4})$ with
$A=\frac{1}{2\pi}$, $c=\frac{1-\log(8\pi)}{2\pi}$,
$d=\frac{1}{48}-\frac{1}{4\pi^2}$,
$e=\frac{4+6\zeta(3)-\pi^2}{96\pi^3}$, and
$f_3=\frac{3\pi^2-36-24\zeta(3)}{576\pi^4}$, with \emph{all}
$\log^k y/y^m$ ($k\ge 1$) terms canceling identically through $m=1,2,3$ \C.
\item In the $s\to\infty$ ($u\downarrow 0$, $y\downarrow 0$) phonon-first regime,
Stirling's series gives $g(s)=1+\frac{\pi}{6s}+\frac{\pi^2}{72s^2}+O(s^{-3})$,
whence $F(u)=\sqrt{\pi}u+\frac{\pi^{3/2}}{12}u^2+O(u^3)$,
$J(u)=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}u^2+\frac{\pi^{3/2}}{36}u^3+O(u^4)$, and
$u(y)=\frac{4}{3\sqrt{\pi}}y^{3/2}-\frac{4}{27\sqrt{\pi}}y^3+O(y^{9/2})$.
Substituting into $C_E(y)$ and $C_v(y)$ gives the small-$y$ expansions
\begin{equation}
 C_E(y) = \frac{3}{4\pi} - \frac{3}{10}y + \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi}}y^{3/2} + O(y^3), \qquad
 C_v(y) = \frac{3}{4\pi} - \frac{1}{2}y + \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}y^{3/2} + O(y^3), \label{eq:CE_Cv_small_y}
\end{equation}
so $C_E(0^+)=C_v(0^+)=\frac{3}{4\pi}$, differing from the $y\to\infty$ limit
$\frac{1}{2\pi}$ by $\frac{1}{4\pi}$ \C. Claim~4 is qualified only because
\scr{uniform\_limits\_analytic.py} checked $C_v(0^+)$ symbolically without
deriving the small-$y$ series of $J(u(y))/F(u(y))$ for $C_E(0^+)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Section 4: Conditional Proposition 1 and Endpoint Moment}
\paragraph{Claims 5, 6, and 7 (\C\ conditional / \D, Qualified / Confirmed).}
Using the exact measure transformation $z=u(x)$ with $B(z)\,dz=2\sqrt{x}\,dx$
and $x=Yt^2$ ($dx=2Yt\,dt$), the half-line convolution is
\begin{equation}
 Q(u(Y),u(r^2Y)) = 8Y^{3/2}\int_0^r t^2 f\bigl(u(Y)-u(r^2Y)+u(Yt^2)\bigr)\,dt. \label{eq:Q_transformed}
\end{equation}
For $r\in(0,1)$ in a compact set, $\sigma=1-r^2+t^2\ge 1-r^2>0$ keeps the
argument $W=u(Y)-u(r^2Y)+u(Yt^2)$ of order $Y$ across the \emph{entire}
interval $t\in[0,r]$, including the endpoint layer $t\in[0,L/\sqrt{Y}]$.
Writing $W=u(x_W)$ and solving for the dressed coordinate $x_W$ at fixed $t>0$
gives
\begin{equation}
 x_W = Y\sigma + \Delta_0 + \frac{1}{Y}\left[\frac{A\Delta_0}{\sigma} + d\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{r^2}+\frac{1}{t^2}-\frac{1}{\sigma}\Bigr)\right] + O(Y^{-2}), \qquad \Delta_0 = -A\log\frac{t^2}{r^2\sigma}, \label{eq:xW_expansion}
\end{equation}
where both $\log Y$ and $c$ cancel out of $\Delta_0$. Substituting
\eqref{eq:xW_expansion} into $f(u(x_W))=x_W^{-1/2}[1-(2d+3A^2)/x_W^2+O(x_W^{-3})]$
and expanding $8Y^{3/2}t^2[f(u(x_W))-(Y\sigma)^{-1/2}]$ in SymPy reproduces
$I_0(t)$ and $I_1(t)$ in Eqs.~(16)--(17) of \scr{uniform\_limits.tex}
identically, and 60-digit quadrature on $[0,r]$ matches the closed forms $c_0(r)$
and $c_1(r)$ to $<10^{-54}$ \C. Splitting at $t=L/\sqrt{Y}$ bounds both the
inner contribution ($\int_0^{L/\sqrt{Y}}dt=O(Y^{-1/2})$ multiplied by the
$O(Y^{-1})$ outer $d$-term, and $\int_0^{L/\sqrt{Y}}t^2\log^m Y\,dt=O(Y^{-3/2}\log^m Y)$)

\section{Failures Recorded}

In accordance with \scr{AGENTS.md}, we record both the package/historical
discrepancies asserted by the audit script and the intermediate check failures
encountered and fixed while developing \scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Historical Phase~2d step-bound failure (asserted in \scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py}):}
Three steps in \scr{toda\_edge\_twoterm.json} exceed the claimed $0.004$ uniform
bound ($0.006326$, $0.009016$, $0.004512$), with linear-$h$ step ratio $0.500415$.
\item \textbf{Historical Phase~2f $1/Y$ sign-definiteness failure (asserted in \scr{audit\_uniform\_limits.py}):}
Across $(h,Y)$ windows and extrapolation bases on the saved finite-sea data, the
fitted $1/Y$ coefficient changes sign both before and after subtracting
$\Phi_{\rm lat}/h$.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 1 (fixed):}
In \texttt{toda\_E0\_minus\_bP0\_series}, our initial hand-calculated $\kappa^7$
coefficient of $E_0(\kappa)-bP_0(\kappa)$ was typed as $\frac{1}{42}b^2\kappa^7$
instead of $\bigl(\frac{8}{315}-\frac{1}{210}\bigr)b^2\kappa^7=\frac{13}{630}b^2\kappa^7$.
SymPy caught the mismatch immediately; corrected to $\frac{13}{630}$.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 2 (fixed):}
In \texttt{fixed\_Y\_shrinking\_momentum}, \texttt{sp.series(..., h, 0, 3).removeO()}
retained both $O(h^{3/2})$ and $O(h^{5/2})$ because $5/2<3$, whereas our initial
assertion subtracted only the leading $\frac43 b^{-1/2}(hY)^{3/2}$ term. Updated
to verify both the $h^{3/2}$ and $\frac{2}{15}b^{-3/2}(hY)^{5/2}$ terms.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 3 (fixed):}
In \texttt{kernel\_G\_asymptotic\_L100} and \texttt{kernel\_R\_asymptotic\_L100},
our initial asymptotic test used alternating signs at $L^{-4},L^{-6},\dots$,
forgetting that $i^{2m}=(-1)^m$ combines with $B_{2m}=(-1)^{m-1}|B_{2m}|$ to make
every term in $R(L)=\sum_{m\ge 1}\frac{|B_{2m}|}{mL^{2m}}$ positive (and every
subleading term in $G(L)$ negative), and required the $m=5$ term
$\frac{1}{66L^{10}}$ at $L=100$ to pass the strict $10^{-22}$ threshold without
weakening the tolerance.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 4 (fixed):}
In \texttt{cusp\_integral\_asymptotic}, evaluating $C_{\rm cusp}$ with
\texttt{mp.quad(..., [1, 4, 20, mp.inf])} suffered catastrophic cancellation in
$R(u)=2\operatorname{Re}\psi(1+iu)-2\log u$ at the transformed infinity nodes
$u>10^{30}$ (giving a $2.47\times 10^{-3}$ error at $h=10^{-2}$). Splitting at
$u_0=10$ and summing the convergent Bernoulli tail
$\sum_{m\ge 2}\frac{|B_{2m}|}{m(2m-2)u_0^{2m-2}}$ restored 40-digit accuracy.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 5 (fixed):}
In \texttt{audit\_wh\_reversion\_and\_matched\_proof}, differentiating
$\Gamma(\nu)^{-1}$ symbolically left unexpanded half-integer polygammas
$\psi^{(r)}(\frac52-k)$ and unsplit $\log(8\pi)$ vs $3\log 2+\log\pi$, and our
initial $J(u(y))$ formula omitted the $-\frac{8A}{9}y^{3/2}$ term and factor of
$2$ in $-\frac{8d}{3}y^{1/2}+\frac{16e}{3}y^{-1/2}$ (since
$\frac{16}{9}\cdot(-\frac{3d}{2})=-\frac{8d}{3}$). Implementing the exact
half-integer polygamma recurrence from $\nu=\frac12$ and the $\frac{16}{9}$
prefactor resolved both checks identically.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 6 (fixed):}
In \texttt{audit\_independent\_wh\_convolution}, our first attempt used a fixed
Talbot contour wrapping into $\operatorname{Re}s<-2\pi$ and a factor of $4w^2$
instead of $2\,dF=8w^2\,dw$. Because $\Gamma(1+s/(2\pi))$ has poles at
$s=-2\pi n$ on the negative real axis, the Talbot contour crossed those poles
and gave an $O(10)$ error at $Y=16$. Switching to de Hoog--Knight--Stokes
Fourier--Pad\'e inversion along a vertical line in $\operatorname{Re}s>0$ and
using $8w^2\,dw$ reproduced $u(Y)$ to $<10^{-24}$ and $R(Y,r)$ to $<1.1\times 10^{-8}$.
\item \textbf{Audit script development failure 7 (fixed):}
In \texttt{FejerTodaSea}, our initial prototype solved $\rho(q)=0$ in hyperbolic
rapidity coordinates rather than the unit-density normalization
$\int_{-q}^q\rho(x)\,dx=1$ in bare momentum $x\in[-q,q]$ with bare energy
$x^2/2$. Enforcing $\int_{-q}^q\rho(x)\,dx=1$ on Fej\'er Chebyshev nodes
matched the Gauss--Legendre Fermi momentum and one-body energy to $<2\times 10^{-7}$.
\end{enumerate}

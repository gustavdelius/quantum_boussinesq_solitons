\begin{abstract}
\C This note preserves the historical files and records corrections from
\file{paper/review_draft1.tex}, section A, \file{phase2/audit_phase2i.tex},
and \file{phase0/reference_audit.md}. It distinguishes corrected mathematical
identities from conditional evidence and unresolved physical identifications.
The bad-equation Wick boundary energy changes sign; the displayed good-soliton
energy and several printed Phase 2i formulas change. The inspected historical
zero-scheme energy tables and executable classical scattering phase do not
change as a consequence of these corrections. Negative-orientation defect
labels require repair. No regularisation, equation, or quantum frame is selected.
\end{abstract}
